Federated Network Policy

1. Respectful Commerce

GrepGoods is a community space. We expect all sellers to interact respectfully with buyers and our automated indexing service. Harassment, spam, or abusive behavior will lead to a permanent ban from the index.

2. Ethical Federation

We believe in the sovereignty of Fediverse instances. We will not index content from instances that are known to host or promote hate speech, harassment, or illegal activities. We actively maintain a blocklist to protect our users.

3. Open Source & Transparency

GrepGoods is built with open-source tools and principles. Our architecture, including our use of local AI and privacy proxies, is designed to be transparent and auditable by the community.

4. Reporting

If you encounter a listing that violates our terms or conduct policy, please report it via your home instance or by contacting the admin at admin@grepgoods.space.
